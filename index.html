About the channel

The Bobo Bear World is a YouTube channel with short, friendly videos for toddlers and preschoolers: counting, colours, shapes, sounds and everyday routines, told by Bobo the bear.

Watch on YouTube: @TheBoboBearWorld

About the uploader app

The channel owner uses a small private tool, "The Bobo Bear World Uploader", to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. The tool is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information about anyone.
