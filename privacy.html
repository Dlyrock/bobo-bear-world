Privacy Policy

The Bobo Bear World Uploader · Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how "The Bobo Bear World Uploader" (the "App") handles information. The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Bobo Bear World to upload the channel's own videos.

What the App does

The App uploads videos created by the channel owner to the owner's own YouTube channel and sets their title, description, tags, audience setting and scheduled publish time. It uses YouTube API Services with a single permission (OAuth scope): https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload.

Information the App accesses

  • The App only receives an access authorization (OAuth token) from Google that lets it upload videos to the channel owner's account.
  • The App does not read, collect, store or share any personal information about viewers, children or any other person.
  • The App does not use cookies, analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind.

How information is stored

The authorization token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, so the App can keep uploading without asking again. It is never sent to any server other than Google's.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share information with anyone. Videos are sent only to YouTube, as requested by the channel owner.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled by Google is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops the App from uploading.

Children

The channel's videos are made for children and are marked as "made for kids" on YouTube. The App and this website do not collect any information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions can be sent through the contact options on the channel's YouTube page.
